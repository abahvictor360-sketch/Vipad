Open Vipad

Step 01

Get started

Open Vipad from your desktop, phone or the web app. The start screen gives you three ways in:

  1. Record — capture from your microphone.
  2. Open files — WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC, M4A, WebM and more. You can also drag files anywhere onto the window.
  3. Generate — create tones, noise or DTMF beeps.

Need the app? Get it from the download page.

Step 02

Record audio

  1. Press the red Record button (or the R key).
  2. Pick your microphone, mono or stereo, and where the take should go: a new file, inserted at the cursor, or overdubbed on top.
  3. Press Record. Watch the level meter — keep peaks out of the red.
  4. Use Pause / Resume as needed, then Stop.

Your recording is saved to your device every two seconds while you record, so even a crash or a dead battery can’t lose it.

Step 03

Select & edit

  1. Drag across the waveform to select. Drag the lime edges to fine-tune, or type exact times in Start / End / Length at the bottom.
  2. Use Cut, Copy, Paste, Delete, Trim (keep only the selection) or Silence.
  3. Zoom with +/−, Ctrl+scroll, or pinch on touch screens. The strip above the waveform is an overview you can drag.
  4. Made a mistake? Undo (Ctrl+Z) — every step is reversible.

Step 04

Apply effects

  1. Select part of the audio (or nothing, to process the whole file).
  2. Choose an effect from the Levels, Effects or Restore tabs, the side panel, or the ☰ menu.
  3. Adjust the sliders and press Preview to hear it before committing.
  4. Press Apply. Don’t like it? Undo.

Highlights: Normalize, Compressor, 10-band EQ, Reverb, Echo, Change Tempo (keeps pitch), Change Pitch (keeps length) and Vocal Remover.

Step 05

Clean up noise

  1. Select a short part that contains only background noise (no voice).
  2. Open ☰ → Restore → Grab Noise Sample.
  3. Select the part to clean (or nothing for the whole file).
  4. Choose Apply Subtraction from Noise Sample, preview and apply.

Also in Restore: Auto Spectral Subtraction, Multi-band Noise Gating, Click/Pop Removal (auto and manual), Hum Removal and DC Offset Correction.

Step 06

Multitrack mixing

  1. Flip the Multitrack switch in the top bar. Flip it off any time to return to the waveform editor — your tracks are kept.
  2. Add tracks with Add File as Track, Import or Record Track (you’ll hear the other tracks while recording — use headphones).
  3. Each track has Mute, Solo, volume and pan. Drag clips to move them in time or onto another track; S splits at the cursor.
  4. Press Mixdown to render everything to one file, or Export Mix.

Step 07

Export & share

  1. Press Export (Ctrl+E).
  2. Pick a format: MP3, M4A (AAC), OGG/WebM (Opus) for small files; WAV, FLAC or AIFF for full quality; or AU / RAW PCM / telephony µ-law and A-law.
  3. Choose Mono or Stereo, a sample rate (10–225 kHz; some formats have fixed rates) and the quality (bitrate or bit depth).
  4. Open Metadata to add title, artist, album, year, genre and comment tags.
  5. Press Export. On phones you’ll get the share sheet to save or send it.

Step 08

Never lose your work

Vipad saves everything on your device automatically — watch the Saved indicator in the top bar.

  • Close the app by mistake, refresh, or crash? Reopen Vipad and your files and multitrack session come back exactly as they were.
  • Interrupted recordings are rebuilt automatically.
  • Closed a file? Find it in ☰ → File → Recently Closed (or Tools → Library) for 30 days and press Restore.
  • Want a permanent copy? Save (Ctrl+S) to your Library or Save Project to a .vipad file.

Step 09

Settings

Open ☰ → Tools → Settings (Ctrl+,) to set defaults for new files, recording (channels, voice processing, play-along), export format and quality, undo levels, follow-playhead and more. Settings are remembered on your device.

Step 10

Keyboard shortcuts

Press ? in the app for the full list. The essentials: Space play/pause · R record · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+X/C/V cut/copy/paste · Del delete · Ctrl+T trim · M add marker · L loop.

Step 11

On phones & tablets

Everything works by touch: drag to select, drag the edges to adjust, pinch to zoom, and swipe the toolbar sideways for more tools. The ☰ menu holds every command. On iPhone/iPad, add Vipad to your Home Screen from Safari for a full-screen app that works offline.

Step 12

Updates, shortcuts & windows

  • Updates: Vipad tells you when a new version is out. Desktop and Android apps show a banner (and a system notification) with a Download button; the web app shows Update now. Check any time from Help → Check for Updates. Turn notifications off in Settings.
  • Shortcut: Help → Create Shortcut / Install App puts Vipad on your desktop or home screen. Right-click (desktop) or long-press (phone) the icon for quick actions: New recording, Multitrack, Library.
  • Window menu: switch between open files (Ctrl+Alt+←/→), jump to the multitrack mixer, show or hide the menu bar, toolbar, side panel and spectrum, go full screen (F11), or reset the layout.

Step 13

Spectrogram, analysis & loudness

  • View → Spectrogram shows frequencies over time — perfect for spotting hum, hiss, clicks and breaths. Switch back with Waveform. Amp +/− (or Alt+scroll) zooms vertically; the dB ruler on the right shows levels.
  • Tools → Analysis graphs the frequency spectrum of your selection and shows the dominant pitch and musical note, integrated loudness (LUFS), loudness range and true peak.
  • Levels → Loudness normalizes to a standard: −14 LUFS (Spotify/YouTube), −16 (Apple Podcasts), −23 (broadcast), with a true-peak ceiling so it never clips.
  • New repair tools: De-clip, De-ess and Shorten Pauses (removes long silences), plus Flanger and Phaser effects.

Step 14

Split, join & presets

  • Tools → Split Here cuts the file in two at the cursor; At Markers makes one file per marked part; By Silence splits a long recording at every pause (great for interviews and lectures).
  • Join All glues every open file together in tab order. Insert File drops another recording in at the cursor. Edit → Extract Left/Right Channel pulls one side of a stereo file.
  • Every effect has Presets — start from built-ins like Voice clarity or Podcast, then Save preset to keep your own.
  • History (side panel) lists every edit — click any step to jump back to it. Edit → Snap to Zero Crossings makes cuts click-free.
  • Recording: turn on a countdown or sound-activated recording (records only while there is sound) in Settings → Recording.

Step 15

Vipad Link: audio over the network

Use a phone as a wireless microphone, or send audio from one computer to another — on the same Wi-Fi or over the internet — and use it as a microphone in Zoom, OBS, Discord or your DAW.

  1. On the computer that should receive: Tools → Vipad Link → Start receiving. A QR code and a 6-letter code appear.
  2. On the phone, tablet or computer that should send: scan the QR code (or open Vipad → Vipad Link → Send and type the code), choose Voice, Music or Hi-fi quality and press Connect and send. Nothing needs installing on the sender.
  3. The receiver presses Accept. Each sender gets a level meter, latency read-out, volume, mute and disconnect.
  4. Use it as a microphone in other apps: Windows — install the free VB-CABLE, then choose “CABLE Output” as the mic. macOS — install the free BlackHole 2ch and choose it as the mic. Linux desktop app — press Create Vipad Link microphone. Vipad sends into the virtual microphone automatically.
  5. Turn on Record guests in Vipad to record senders with the normal Record button.
  6. VBAN (desktop app): exchange audio with Voicemeeter, VBAN Receptor and VBAN Talkie on your network.
  7. Devices on strict networks that can’t connect directly can use a TURN relay (Settings → Vipad Link). On iPhone and iPad keep the screen on while sending.
