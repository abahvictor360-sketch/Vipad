About Vipad

Vipad is a complete sound studio in a single app. Record voice-overs, podcasts, lectures, interviews and music, then cut, join, clean and polish them with professional-grade tools.

Every edit is non-destructive with unlimited undo, every effect can be previewed before you commit, and everything is processed locally on your device — fast, private and fully usable offline.

Questions, answered

Is Vipad really free?

Yes. Every feature is free on every platform, with no watermarks, time limits or sign-up. If Vipad helps you, you can support its development.

Does my audio get uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording, editing and effects all run locally on your device. Recordings are saved in a private on-device library.

Which file formats are supported?

Vipad opens WAV, AIFF, AU, MP3, OGG, FLAC, M4A/AAC, WebM and Opus, and saves as MP3, M4A (AAC), OGG & WebM (Opus), WAV (incl. 24-bit, 32-bit float, µ-law, A-law), FLAC, AIFF, AU and RAW PCM — mono or stereo, 10–225 kHz, with title/artist/album tags.

Can I use my phone as a microphone for my computer?

Yes, with Vipad Link. On the computer choose Tools → Vipad Link → Start receiving, then scan the QR code with your phone and press Connect. To use it in other apps, install the free VB-CABLE (Windows) or BlackHole (macOS); on Linux the desktop app creates the microphone for you. See the guide.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. The desktop and Android apps work fully offline, and the web app caches itself after your first visit so it keeps working without a connection.

How do I install on iPhone or iPad?

Open Vipad in Safari, tap the Share button and choose “Add to Home Screen”. It launches full-screen like a native app and works offline.

Windows or macOS says the app is from an unknown developer.

Early builds are not yet code-signed. On Windows choose “More info → Run anyway”; on macOS right-click the app and choose “Open”.
