Schools, studios, radio stations, podcast networks and companies that rely on Vipad can sponsor its development.

What sponsors get

  • Your logo on the Vipad website and download page, linked to your site
  • A say in the roadmap — priority on the features your team needs
  • Direct contact for deployment questions across your organisation
  • Invoices and receipts for your accounts team
  • Custom work — sponsored features, integrations or branding, by arrangement

Everything Vipad builds stays free for everyone. Sponsorship never buys access to anyone’s recordings — Vipad never sees them.

Questions

Do I need to pay to use Vipad?

No. Every feature is free on every platform, with no time limits, watermarks or sign-up. Support is entirely optional.

Is my payment secure?

Payments are handled by GitHub, Paystack or Flutterwave on their own secure pages. Vipad never sees or stores your card or bank details.

Can I cancel a monthly contribution?

Yes, any time, from the platform you used to give. There’s nothing to cancel in Vipad itself.

Can my business get an invoice?

Yes. Send a sponsorship enquiry above and mention the details your accounts team needs.
